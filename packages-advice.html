Packages & Advice for Your Stay

Compare tailored stay packages, find out which room option fits your schedule in Singapore, and prepare your enquiry with confidence.

Choose a Stay Package

Every package includes utilities, high-speed Wi-Fi, key-card security, and access to shared resident spaces.

Academic Term

Semester & Academic Package

Designed for university students studying in Singapore for 1 or 2 semesters.

  • Accommodation: Campus Room (private bedroom, shared kitchen & lounge)
  • Included: High-speed Wi-Fi, water, electricity & weekly common-area cleaning
  • Extras: 24/7 access to quiet study rooms & resident social events
  • Enquiry Tip: Provide your university name & semester dates
Most Flexible

Internship & Placement Package

Built for interns and professionals on fixed-term work or training placements.

  • Accommodation: Choice of Campus Room or Independent Studio
  • Included: Utilities, high-speed Wi-Fi, key-card access & study/work lounges
  • Extras: Flexible contract dates matched to your placement letter
  • Enquiry Tip: Include exact arrival and departure dates
Tailored Arrangements

Corporate & Group Package

Coordinated housing for university cohorts, visiting researchers, or employer teams.

  • Accommodation: Block allocation of Campus Rooms and/or Studios
  • Included: Full building facilities, laundry, fitness room & bicycle storage
  • Extras: Dedicated coordinator & custom individual accessibility support
  • Enquiry Tip: Share group size, room mix & any access requirements

Recommended Option

Campus Room (Semester & Academic Package)

Ideal if you want your own lockable furnished bedroom while enjoying a social student community with shared kitchen and dining spaces.

Private vs. Shared Breakdown:
  • Private: Your furnished bedroom & study desk
  • Shared: Apartment kitchen, dining lounge, quiet study rooms & gym
  • Include in Enquiry: Course start/end dates & preferred move-in week
Recommended Option

Placement Stay (Internship & Placement Package)

Built for temporary work or study placements where contract length is agreed directly with SkyNest Co-Living Space around your placement dates.

Private vs. Shared Breakdown:
  • Private: Furnished bedroom (en-suite studio option available)
  • Shared: Study/work lounges, courtyard, laundry & fitness room
  • Include in Enquiry: Exact arrival and departure dates
Recommended Option

Independent Studio

Best for residents who want their own self-contained bathroom and kitchenette while still enjoying full access to SkyNest's shared building facilities.

Private vs. Shared Breakdown:
  • Private: Bedroom, en-suite bathroom & personal kitchenette
  • Shared: Fitness room, indoor courtyard, laundry & bicycle storage
  • Include in Enquiry: Target move-in date & intended stay duration
Recommended Option

Group & Individual Arrangements

University cohorts, employer teams, or individuals needing specific room adjustments or step-free access can arrange availability directly with our team.

How to Arrange:
  • Confirmation: Availability and arrangements are confirmed directly
  • Facilities: Reception support, key-card access & flexible room layouts
  • Include in Enquiry: Group size, dates & specific access needs
